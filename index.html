Julián Fernández Bonder

Professor · Math Department, Universidad de Buenos Aires
Principal Researcher · CONICET at Instituto de Cálculo

I am a Professor at the Math Department in the Faculty of Exact and Natural Sciences at Universidad de Buenos Aires, and a Principal Researcher at CONICET, based at the Instituto de Cálculo.

My current research focuses on nonlinear and nonlocal elliptic-type equations, including problems in Orlicz–Sobolev spaces, fractional operators, shape optimization, and eigenvalue problems for quasilinear operators.
